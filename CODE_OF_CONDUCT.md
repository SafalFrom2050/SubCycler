# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome questions and feedback from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep criticism specific to the work and explain the changes you suggest.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation. Use GitHub's reporting tools to report harassment, threats, or other abuse privately.
