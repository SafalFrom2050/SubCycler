# SubCycler 🔄 — Brand & Marketing Playbook

> Strategic positioning, marketing angles, copy bank, and viral growth hooks for **SubCycler: The Agentic 45-Day Subscription Cycler & Auto-Cancel Co-Pilot**.

---

## 🎯 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

* **App Name**: `SubCycler` 🔄
* **Core Philosophy**: **Intentional Streaming over Passive Inaction**. Corporate subscription models rely on customer inertia (auto-billing people during weeks they are too busy to watch). SubCycler hands the control back to the user with smart rotation cycles and autonomous AI cancellations.
* **Tone of Voice**: Modern, smart, anti-subscription fatigue, funky, punchy, and transparent (100% local-first & open-source).

---

## 🚀 4. Viral Growth & Distribution Channels

### A. Reddit Strategy (Community-First)
**Target Subreddits**:
* `r/cordcutters` (2M+ members)
* `r/frugal` (3M+ members)
* `r/SideProject` & `r/selfhosted`
* `r/Android` & `r/androidapps`

**High-Converting Post Template**:
> **Title**: *I built an open-source Android app that rotates streaming services on a 45-day cycle (30d active + 15d blackout) to cut subscription bills in half.*
>
> **Body**: Most streaming apps rely on us forgetting to cancel during busy weeks. I realized I don't watch Netflix during the last 2 weeks of the month anyway, so I built **SubCycler**. You binge for 30 days, the app alerts you on Day 28 to turn off auto-renew, and reminds you on Day 45 when your blackout ends and you're ready to resubscribe. It also pairs 2 services to alternate seamlessly. 100% offline, native Jetpack Compose, no account required.
